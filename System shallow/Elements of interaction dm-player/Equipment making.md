# Craftsmanship/ Smithing

when player wants to craft object related to wood/leather (craftsmanship) or metal (smithing) they must use the craftsmans station and/or smtithing station and declare how much and which elements they want to use. 
	every element repersents a factor in crafting :
	1. Red  - ferocity/power
	2. Green - flexibility/control
	3. Yellow - sturdiness/durability
	4. Blue -finesse/precision
	5. White -purity/empowerment
	6. Black - corrpution/cruelty

# Alchemy 

Alchemy is the mechanic of potion brewing and mixing them together.
	Creating potions
	potions are crearted from elements by mixing them together in a cauldron or brewing stand.
		Elements have their values on 3rd dim grid (blood/water,plant/rock,life/death)
		1. Red - (1,0,0)
		2. Blue -(-1,0,0)
		3. Green (0,1,0)
		4. yellow (0,-1,0)
		5. White (0,0,1)
		6. black (0,0,-1)
		[[List of potions|potions]]
		- Mixing potions
			there are two types of mixing potions depending on how many of them you wish to mix.:
			1. 2 or 3 - simple mixing | made by simply adding the values of the potions in the mix ^6d895f
			2. 4 or more - complex mixing | made by multiplication of matrixes made from potions values
