All adventures and quests result in earining money, finding treasures and valuables. But without economy of the world they become utterly useless.
1. Soruces of price
	1. Elements 
	   All objects can be disassembled into  6 basic elements, by which their value can be set
	2. Supply and demand
	   depending on the supply and demand in certain areas some things may change their value
	3. Quality of service 
	   depening on how good object/service is it can raise or drop in value.
	4. Culture and politics
	   Depending on how the law and culture is established in the region the price can variate
	5. Barnding and status
	   objects bought from famous craftsman can both have higher quality and price often exciding the quality
	6. History
	   some items are worth more just because they where significant artifacts.
	7. intellectual properies.
	   the idea or the patent may be exclusive to the shop, which raises the price
2. Types of currency 
	1. Barter - object /service for an object/service
	   most commonly used in rural or primal palces where everyone in small collective agrees how much certain things are worth 
	2. Coin manufacutres 
	   Coins in LOS are in certain order where we go from least to most valuable
		1. copper - lowest coin
		2. iron - 100 copper
		3. silver -10 iron
		4. gold - 10 silver
		5. platinum - 100 gold
	3. bank notes - paper cash based on ores
	   clinet deposits the copper/iron/silver/gold/platinum and recives a bank note with how much is ti worth, the they can withdraw it at any bank at need.
3. Monetary system
	1. Shallow (domain)
	   All cash in  Shallow is almost the same. Every country has their own coins but the raising is the same everywhere.
		1. Empire 
		   all 5 types of coins uses I- in front to not be mistaken in character sheet (eg. Imerial copper coin is Ec , and golden coin is Eg)
		2. Red Seketh 
		   same as emire using R- in front the same way
		3. Kagane no Kuni
		   using only copper, iron and gold coins and using K- at the front (1Kg = 10 Ki = 1000 Kc)
		4. Astrala 
		   uses only copper, silver and golden coins using A- at front (1Ag=10As=1000Ac)
		5. Northen Union
			uses coins like empire for identification uses N- at front
