# fate
Fate dice has an significant meaning towards the whole game

when the ammount of points that player achived isn't satisfactory player can reroll a amount of dice that  is restricted to ammount of dice they have designated by staistics and bonuses

after that they roll a **FATE DICE** and repect the value as it shows:


| number on dice | effect           |
| -------------- | ---------------- |
| 1              | critical fail    |
| 2,3,4          | 0                |
| 5,6,7          | +1 point         |
| 8              | critical success |

## <b><font color="#ff0000">Critical Fail</font> </b>

## <b><font color="#ffff00">Critical Succes</font> </b>

when player recievs that they immedietly win with even better outcome than normally if they won. DM let's get creative 






# Blessings


Blessings and curses are a way for DM to reward or punish players with ==TEMPORARY== reward/punishment, unlike talents they will vanish after a certain ammount of uses and ==CAN'T== be permament unless necessitated by the plot or chosen during character creation.
### scroll blessings and curses
 Blesses and Curses can be found in the scrolls as well, but then they need to be randomized using number assigned to the blesses and curses
 player rolls d6 and d100
 d6 - odd = curse
 d6- even = bless
 where the d100 indicates the number of the curse/blessing
