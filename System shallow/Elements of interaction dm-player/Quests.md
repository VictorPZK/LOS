## Arenas

arena is a type of entretainment for players that vary depending on the type of arena

### <b> <font color="#b2a2c7">arena types </b></font>

1. Clasic arena:
		 Battle goes in respected order:
			1.  team vs team battle
			2. arena shop 
			3. team vs monster
			4. arena rest
2. anti magical arena:
		Battle same as  classic ones but can't use offensive magic		
3. puzzle tower:
			no fioghts just puzzles
4. reflection camertors:
			randomises crystal reflections and replay battles (DM have fun when players skipped your boss
5. Elemental trial:
			Magic is restricted to certain limits to test the special combinations and try new combinations
6. Labirynth:
			moving walls, many enemies, bullet hell and complete destruction (Disclamer! it's the hard one)
