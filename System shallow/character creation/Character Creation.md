### Looks 
despite it being trival at first how character looks has a meaning besides lore aspects of the character

Few of those characteristisc include :
1. Height 
   character won't be able to fit in all doorways and passages if it's to big in exchange of easier climbing and bigger reach in both movement and combat
2. weight
   Every 5 kg of mass counts as 1 slot in inventory in case someone must carry the character, making it harder to evacuate you in case you need to be carried.
3. age 
   some actions , like buying alcohol or visiting certin places has an age restriction for characters. but in compensation, playing under age has it's benefits (eg. NPC will be more likely to trust you and save you from danger unless you give them reason to not trust you.)
4. racial dynamics 
   some races live in harmony while fighting others . this makes interactions with npcs who are baised harder and more intense. (eg. Elves and Dvares are more likely to attack one another provoked than human and halflings)

#### Elenemtal infusion
When two or more elemenets are added to one bending there proceedes: 
Elemental infusion works on specified rules:
1. every time there are two or more elements used in the bending they always infuse.
	1. elements may be drawn from enviroment in certain situations
2. all elements used for singular bending must be casted during one turn
	1. only exception is an area of effect spell that lasts more than a round, it can mix with new spell that enter it's range
3. the reaction is read from left to right 
4. elements are paired if possible and go through the fusion in those pairs
5. if irregulrar ammount of elements is infused the ones without a pair are moved to next line of reactions
6. after first fusion the process repeats untill all elements are considered into one bending technique
7. maximum size of infusion is 6 elements
	1.  bigger fusions are allowed ,but due to magic instability after 3rd fusing every next step of fusing gives a chance for it to collapse creating an elemental
8. if fusion doesn't exist and can't work the spell fall apart
9. first combination of elements in creases the test lvl by (example. if caster uses fire+fire for the  frist time), every next one is treated normally

## Knowledge
Knowledge is an innate passive skill that cannot be honed by basic leveling, it provides the player with lore information usually  unavailable for their character through observation/intuition/religion/arcane tests.
Each player begins the game with knowledge relative to their background and class and can advance in any field by learning the necessary theory through reading , experience or obtaining a specific class.

Just like in the real world, it is spread into specific categories such as:
1. History - awareness of society, buildings , places and artifacts in a region.
2. Law - awareness of the law and its inner workings in a region.
3. Anatomy - awareness of how the body of a species works.
4. Medicine - awareness of how to treat a spiecies, their wounds or any ailments they may posess.
5. Botanics - awaress of how to cultivate, differentiate and take care of plantlife.
6. Herbalism - awareness of which plants have medicinal properties and how dangerous they might be.
7. Economy - awareness of how trade and exchange works, the intricacies of attaining wealth.
8. Necromancy - awareness of how undead and their bodies are made as well as how to control them.
9. Demonology - awareness of the existence of demons and how they function, as well as general knowledge about their society and culture.
10. Engineering - awareness of the laws of physics, construction methods ,material strenght as well as how to create and maintain constructs.
11. Geography - awareness of the surrounding terrain and the ability to create maps.
12. Mathematics - awareness of mathematical formulas, ability to recognize patterns of the universe, ability to quickly count.
13. Abyssalinite - awareness of how the abyss works, and how to forge abyssalinite.
14. Theology - awarness of the world's holy law, its gods and the heirearchy of the universe.
15. Divinity - awareness of how holy magic works, and how to become a god or demigod.
16. Divination - awareness of the inner working of fate and how to predict it through astrology, cards, crystal balls, bones, and spiritual items.

# Combat
 in combat every player and enemy has their Initiative and perform turn in order from highest to lowest
## Initiative 
every player rolls d20 for their initiative in combat every turn or can similar to npc  only once  at the start of the battle, but must choose which way they want it.

## Combat turn 
Without any additional skills all characters have one Action and bonus action to perform . Action can be exchanged for two bonus actions.
1. Core Actions:
	1. Attack
	2. Magic cast
	3. ability use
	4. Equipment change
	5. Performing of test (eg. throw.)
	6. getting up (from prone status)
	7. Stabilisng a creature (removing the lethal statuses bringing the target to 1 hp) 
	8. Defense stance (dodges and parries are not limited by a reaction limit)
2.  Core bonus actions
	1. movement (of characters movement range)
	2. prepare for action (using this turn bonus action with next turn one as a full action)
	3. Reaction (max one per turn)
	4. passing the object (to other creature)
	5. Effect removal (if effect is easily removable eg. bleeding)

## Armour class (AC) and Armour Break (AB)
every character has some resistance to recived dmg and ability to break through others defences
after successful hit player checks if they pierce through enemies AC with the table 

| difference in AC ... AB | result            |
| ----------------------- | ----------------- |
| AC <= AB                | 100% dmg inflcted |
| AC > AB by 1            | 90 %              |
| AC > AB by 2            | 80%               |
| AC > AB by 3 - 9        | 70- 10 %          |
| AC > AB by 10           | 0%                |
for every defender AC above atacker's AB reduce dmg by 10%
#### AC
math: $Str,mod * agi,mod/(tl*2)$
and it implies to characters three AC's:
1. melee
2. magical
3. ranged
#### AB
Armour break is directly taken from statistics:
1. melee - str
2. ranged - agi
3. magical - wis
[[#Weaponry|weapon mastery]] affects how much more AB you have, as well as certain weapons give bonus to designated armour types (exp. [[#^1]])

## Special effects
### Mercy Killing

Special trait of stabbing weaponry, it's the ability to strike precisely on crucial points of the body and inflicting deep wounds that can't be stabilised quick enough to stop them from becoming lethal
Such effect on strikes can occur when:
- the victim is ambushed (surpised attacks)
- The assaulter knows where to strike (high weaponry skills)
- attacker knows enemy's weak points ( high medicine/survival knowlage)
target of this effect must perform enurance test (first hard, all further ones one above.) to not bleed out until someone stabilises them, if failed they die

### Dismemberment
Special trait of slashing weaponry, it's the ability to strike with enough precision and force to cut of a limb from the target's body.
Such effect on strikes can occur when :
- target fails to dogde/parry
- attacker is precise in thier attack (precision)
- attacker posseses enough strength to cut (strength and high weaponry skills)

target of this effect must perform enurance test to not bleed out (extrenal bleeding) until someone stabilises them, if failed they die

- Depending on the cut off part - endurace test:

| cut off part | test lvl      |
| ------------ | ------------- |
| toes/fingers | average       |
| hand         | medium        |
| elbow        | hard          |
| whole arm    | very hard     |
| foot         | medium        |
| knee         | difficult     |
| whole leg    | extreme       |
| head         | instant death |

- attacker is precise in thier attack (precision)
- attacker posseses enough strength to cut (strength and high weaponry skills)
target of this effect must perform enurance test to not bleed out (internal bleeding) until someone stabilises them, if failed they die
