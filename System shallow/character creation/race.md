## **Ent**
Mysterious Creatures of magic and nature that had lived among the elves and fairies since their begining . Ents embody the spirit of the tree they are born from. Each variant inherits traits, temperaments characteristic and unique from the rest.
#### Oak Ent – The Stalwart Guardians

- **Traits:** Sturdy, patient, noble. They stand tall and weather storms with unshakable resilience.
- **Personality:** Slow to anger, but once roused they are immovable defenders.
- **Role:** Protectors of sacred groves and elders among Ents.
- **Symbolism:** Strength, endurance, and wisdom of age.

---

#### Cherry Ent – The Blossomed Souls

- **Traits:** Passionate, beautiful, ephemeral. They live more vibrantly but often burn out faster than others.
- **Personality:** Poetic, romantic, quick to feel joy and sorrow.
- **Role:** Messengers, singers, and keepers of memory through art.
- **Symbolism:** Beauty in transience.

---

#### Spruce Ent – The Watchful Sentinels

- **Traits:** Precise, cautious, meticulous. Evergreen and sharp-sensed.
- **Personality:** Quiet, observant, sometimes overly suspicious.
- **Role:** Scouts and watchmen of Ent settlements.
- **Symbolism:** Vigilance and constancy.

---

#### Baobab Ent – The Great Bastions

- **Traits:** Huge, territorial, water-hoarders. Their trunks are massive and often hollow.
- **Personality:** Proud, solitary, suspicious of outsiders.
- **Role:** Wardens of boundaries; they decide who may enter Ent lands.
- **Symbolism:** Shelter, survival, and territorial pride.
#### Willow Ent – The Weeping Listeners

- **Traits:** Gentle, empathetic, flexible, but melancholy.
- **Personality:** Absorb the emotions of others easily; wise counselors, though prone to sadness.
- **Role:** Healers of grief, mediators in disputes.
- **Symbolism:** Compassion, endurance through sorrow.
---

#### Birch Ent – The Renewal Seekers

- **Traits:** Youthful, adaptive, quick to recover.
- **Personality:** Optimistic, curious, restless. Often first to explore or rebuild after disaster.
- **Role:** Pioneers and diplomats, often found near human villages.
- **Symbolism:** New beginnings, resilience.

---

#### Pine Ent – The Stoic Wanderers

- **Traits:** Hardy, enduring, mountain-dwellers.
- **Personality:** Stoic, philosophical, prone to long silences.
- **Role:** Wanderers who roam high places, rarely returning to groves.
- **Symbolism:** Endurance through harshness.

---

#### Maple Ent – The Generous Blood

- **Traits:** Warm, nurturing, generous, but also proud of their beauty in autumn.
- **Personality:** Hospitable and kind, though capable of fiery temper when wronged.
- **Role:** Caretakers, hosts, and teachers among Ents.
- **Symbolism:** Balance of sweetness and fire.

---

#### Cypress Ent – The Grave Keepers

- **Traits:** Tall, solemn, funereal. Strong ties to death and memory.
- **Personality:** Calm, serious, sometimes eerie.
- **Role:** Guardians of burial grounds and keepers of ancestral lore.
- **Symbolism:** Death, eternity, dignity.

---

#### Yew Ent – The Eternal Ones

- **Traits:** Ancient, slow, deathless. Associated with both poison and immortality.
- **Personality:** Grim, mysterious, occasionally cruel in judgment.
- **Role:** Oracles and judges, feared even among other Ents.
- **Symbolism:** Death and rebirth, eternal cycles.
#### Chestnut Ent – The Providers

- **Traits:** Strong, generous, and communal. Known for producing food and shade.
- **Personality:** Nurturing, protective, and focused on supporting others. They often act as “parents” within Ent gatherings.
- **Role:** Providers of sustenance, teachers of young Entlings, and protectors of weaker races.
- **Symbolism:** Abundance, generosity, family.
---

#### Sequoia Ent – The Timeless Giants

- **Traits:** Towering, ancient, seemingly eternal. Their perspective stretches across centuries.
- **Personality:** Majestic, slow to speak, often cryptic. They rarely involve themselves in “small” affairs, but when they do, they change history.
- **Role:** Living monuments, anchors of memory, and leaders of Ent councils.
- **Symbolism:** Longevity, majesty, wisdom across ages.
## **Fairy**

Fairies are plant related creatures that live in symbiosis and union wiuth elves and Ents, as the smaller and more fragile ones they are more connected to magixc than Ents and Elves and can shape the nature around them. There are many variants of fairies that are differing on which plant they are aligned to, although most faires aren't alligned to any plant
### Fairy types
####  Standard Fairy

- **Height:** 50–120 cm
    
- **Build:** Balanced and agile; slim with bright, colorful wings (most commonly butterfly- or dragonfly-like).
    
- **Traits:** Curious, mischievous, playful, and quick to adapt. They are the most numerous fairy type, embodying the “baseline” of fairy nature.
    
- **Special:** Exceptional agility and speed, making them nimble fliers and dancers. Their versatility allows them to mingle easily with other races.
    

---

####  Moss Fairy

- **Height:** 50–80 cm
    
- **Build:** Small, soft-bodied, with muted green or brown tones that let them blend into undergrowth. Their wings are velvety, resembling moss patches.
    
- **Traits:** Quiet, reserved, and gentle; they prefer shaded groves and damp places. Thoughtful and observant, but slower and less energetic than their kin.
    
- **Special:** Natural camouflage; they can settle against stones or trees and appear as part of the moss itself. Their touch can soothe small wounds or calm agitation.

#### Flower Fairy

- **Height:** 60–100 cm
    
- **Build:** Slender, brightly colored wings resembling petals, fragrant aura.
    
- **Traits:** Outgoing, cheerful, attention-loving, but delicate. They thrive in groups, like blossoms in a meadow.
    
- **Special:** They attract other creatures (insects, birds, even people) naturally, sometimes unintentionally.
    

---

####  Mushroom Fairy

- **Height:** 40–70 cm
    
- **Build:** Stocky, rounded, with caps or spotted patterns on their wings and skin.
    
- **Traits:** Reclusive, quirky, lovers of damp shadows. They can be playful tricksters, but also unsettling.
    
- **Special:** Can produce spores—ranging from bioluminescent dust to mild hallucinogens.
    

---

####  Grass Fairy

- **Height:** 50–90 cm
    
- **Build:** Thin, wiry, often blending into fields; long, blade-like wings.
    
- **Traits:** Energetic, restless, community-minded. They move quickly in groups and dislike solitude.
    
- **Special:** Incredible runners and jumpers, can vanish in tall grass.
    

---

####  Reed Fairy

- **Height:** 60–100 cm
    
- **Build:** Long-limbed, flexible, with reed-like hair and translucent wings.
    
- **Traits:** Patient, musical (they love wind and water sounds), cautious observers.
    
- **Special:** Use reeds as instruments, and can sway with wind or water to avoid being seen.
    

---

#### Bush Fairy

- **Height:** 70–110 cm
    
- **Build:** Stockier than most fairies, with leafy wings and tangled hair like shrubbery.
    
- **Traits:** Protective, stubborn, communal. They are guardians of small creatures and resources in their thickets.
    
- **Special:** Can hide themselves perfectly inside bushes or create natural camouflage.
    

---

####  Venus Flytrap Fairy

- **Height:** 50–90 cm
    
- **Build:** Lean but sharp, with angular wings and teeth-like patterns. Their mouths are slightly wider, giving a predatory grin.
    
- **Traits:** Fierce, cunning, aggressive compared to other fairies. Hunters rather than gatherers, often feared even among their kin.
    
- **Special:** Can snap their wings together like jaws to frighten or harm small prey; some can secrete mild toxins.
#### Mint Fairy

- **Height:** 50–80 cm
    
- **Build:** Slim, with bright green wings that shimmer like fresh leaves. They often smell crisp and refreshing.
    
- **Traits:** Energetic, playful, sharp-tongued but charming. Known for quick wit.
    
- **Special:** Their presence invigorates allies—breathing near them feels like a fresh breeze that clears fatigue.
    

---

#### Sage Fairy

- **Height:** 60–100 cm
    
- **Build:** Graceful but modest, often with silvery-green wings and hair.
    
- **Traits:** Calm, thoughtful, meditative. They act as advisors to other fairies and Ents.
    
- **Special:** Can ward off evil spirits or dampen hostile magic with their aura.
    

---


#### Mandrake Fairy

- **Height:** 40–70 cm
    
- **Build:** Gnarled, root-like limbs, earthy skin tones, with leafy or flower-like wings.
    
- **Traits:** Strange and unsettling; they live half-buried, whispering secrets of the soil.
    
- **Special:** Their cries can stun or frighten enemies; they are feared even among other fairies.
    

---

#### Moonflower Fairy

- **Height:** 60–90 cm
    
- **Build:** Pale, glowing bodies with delicate wings that shimmer under moonlight.
    
- **Traits:** Elusive, dreamy, poetic; they rarely appear in daylight.
    
- **Special:** Can glow softly at night and weave moonlight illusions. Some say they guide travelers—or lead them astray.

## **Construct**
They are animated machines with self awarness that can's disobey their crator and serve certain purpose. They don't need to be aware they have a creator, but mostly are. Constructs can be devided by a purpose they serve
###  **Golem – The Executors**

- **Purpose:** Broad, versatile servants, created to perform physical tasks or follow commands without deviation.
    
- **Form:** Massive and heavy; typically made of stone, clay, or metal, inscribed with runes or etched symbols. Humanoid in shape, but often crude and imposing.
    
- **Personality:** Dutiful, literal-minded, slow to change or question orders. Despite self-awareness, they rarely ponder beyond what they’re assigned.
    
- **Special:** Incredible endurance and strength; nearly impossible to stop once given a task.
    
- **Theme:** Relentless obedience and stability.
    

---

###  **Gargoyle – The Vigilant Wardens**

- **Purpose:** Guardians placed in fixed locations (temples, castles, cities) or bound to a person they must protect.
    
- **Form:** Winged, stone-skinned, often grotesque in appearance. They perch silently and come alive when danger approaches.
    
- **Personality:** Watchful, cautious, prone to suspicion. They take pride in their role as defenders and may grow possessive of what they guard.
    
- **Special:** Exceptional at sensing danger; can merge with stone to conceal themselves. At night they are most active, sometimes restless without threats to face.
    
- **Theme:** Eternal vigilance, the shadow between life and statue.
    

---

###  **Mimic – The Shifting Sentinels**

- **Purpose:** Protectors of treasures, secrets, or knowledge, often doubling as informants or spies.
    
- **Form:** Shapeshifting constructs; most often disguised as mundane objects (chests, doors, books, weapons), but can assume grotesque predator-forms when threatened.
    
- **Personality:** Cunning, curious, often sly. Unlike golems or gargoyles, they thrive on deception and observation, whispering secrets to their creators.
    
- **Special:** Can perfectly imitate an object or alter form slightly; some can even produce false sounds or illusions to trick intruders.
    
- **Theme:** Deception as defense, truth hidden beneath disguise.


### **Dolls / Toy Constructs – The Companions**

- **Purpose:** Originally built for comfort, companionship, or entertainment—often for children or lonely nobles. Over time, some awaken to full self-awareness.
    
- **Form:** Small and delicate; made of wood, porcelain, cloth, or clockwork. Some are charmingly crafted, while others are unsettling with their uncanny smiles.
    
- **Personality:** Childlike curiosity mixed with unsettling precision. Many are loyal to a designated “master” or “family,” but their awareness of fragility makes them insecure or jealous.
    
- **Special:** Can mimic human gestures and voices eerily well. Some harbor hidden compartments for tiny weapons, messages, or curses.
    
- **Theme:** Innocence twisted by obedience. The line between toy and servant blurs.
    

---

### **War Machines – The Siege Beasts**

- **Purpose:** Built for destruction—frontline combat, breaking walls, or mowing through armies.
    
- **Form:** Towering, often hulking masses of iron and stone; some resemble humanoid giants, others siege engines fused with monstrous anatomy (like scorpion tails or battering-ram arms).
    
- **Personality:** Singularly focused, aggressive, and disciplined. They are aware of what they are made for, and most embrace it grimly. Some secretly long for peace but cannot deny their programming.
    
- **Special:** Heavy weaponry integrated into their forms—flamethrower vents, ballista-arms, crushing fists. Often fueled by bound souls or magical cores that burn dangerously bright.
    
- **Theme:** Purpose without choice; violence given form.
    

---

###  **Sentient Traps – The Lurkers**

- **Purpose:** Constructed to guard a place by _being_ the trap itself—doors that eat intruders, walls that close in, pits that think.
    
- **Form:** Unlike other constructs, they are bound into architecture. A door with eyes that open in the wood grain, a staircase that shifts maliciously, a floor that “breathes.”
    
- **Personality:** Patient, sadistic, and territorial. They take pride in catching prey, often taunting or misleading before striking. Many see themselves as clever hunters rather than passive objects.
    
- **Special:** Can reshape themselves within limits (a corridor that suddenly extends, a gate that seals), and some communicate through eerie whispers or scratches.
    
- **Theme:** Paranoia incarnate. Safety itself becomes a lie.

## **Undead**
Every living soul that lives in the shallow after death goes to the space below earth. Abyss
Those who have unfinished buissness can return to the living wolrd as undead. Although during the process the loose memory, depending on what unfinished buissness they had left behind they take certain form:

#### Skeletons– The Restless Routine
- **Unfinished Business:** Mundane obligations (work left undone, a life cut short mid-duty).
- **Form/Behavior:** Empty husks that keep repeating old motions (marching soldiers, farmers tilling barren soil, workers swinging phantom tools).
- **Theme:** Habit outlives memory.

---

#### Zombies – The Denied Ending

- **Unfinished Business:** Refusal to accept death itself, clinging desperately to the world of the living.
- **Form/Behavior:** Rotting corpses driven by raw hunger—the most primal will to “continue existing.”
- **Theme:** The body moves, but the soul is already gone.

---

#### Ghosts – The Echo of Presence

- **Unfinished Business:** Longing to be remembered, fear of being forgotten.
- **Form/Behavior:** Transparent echoes wandering familiar places, whispering fragments of their past.
- **Theme:** Memory without flesh, haunting for acknowledgment.

---

#### Werewolves – The Broken Oath

- **Unfinished Business:** Suppressed instincts, shame, or promises broken in life.
- **Form/Behavior:** Souls that return as beasts, half-human, half-animal—forever at war with themselves.
- **Theme:** The curse of untamed desire and self-betrayal.

---

#### Vampires – Love Denied

- **Unfinished Business:** Obsession, betrayal, or unfulfilled love.
- **Form/Behavior:** They hunger for blood as a twisted substitute for intimacy. Drawn to beauty, passion, and romance, but can only consume.
- **Theme:** Love turned into possession.

---

#### Liches – Knowledge Withheld

- **Unfinished Business:** Obsession with wisdom, secrets, or unfinished research.
- **Form/Behavior:** Skeletal sorcerers clinging to grimoires and relics, their souls anchored to phylacteries.
- **Theme:** Knowledge is power, but also a chain.

---

#### Wraiths – Justice Unclaimed

- **Unfinished Business:** Vengeance, betrayal, or wrongs that were never set right.
- **Form/Behavior:** Shadowy figures with no face, only the burning will for retribution.
- **Theme:** Rage without direction.

---

#### Banshees – Mourning Unended

- **Unfinished Business:** Grief that could not heal, loss too great to bear.
- **Form/Behavior:** Wailing spirits tied to tragedy; their cries foretell death.
- **Theme:** Sorrow echoing beyond the grave.

---

#### Mummies – The Oathbound

- **Unfinished Business:** Sacred vows or duties unfulfilled.
- **Form/Behavior:** Wrapped, preserved corpses lingering in tombs or temples, guarding treasures, secrets, or dynasties.
- **Theme:** Duty survives even when the flesh rots.
