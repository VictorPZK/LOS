### description
1. You are able to create potions that increase your skills in a given field. A potion can increase your dice count to a maximum of 2 for a maximum duration of 3 turns/minutes.
2. The number of dice that can be obtained increases to 4 and the time to 9 turns/minutes.

### skill can evolve into three branches

#### Class Potions

3. You can create potions that temporarily grant you skills or knowledge from other classes. A potion will grant you one class skill, depending on the ingredients used (e.g., holy water can be used to create a paladin's potion) for up to 9 minutes. The better the ingredients, the higher the rarity of the skill you can obtain. You can only create potions corresponding to classes up to level 20. And only at the maximum quality level of Rare. Potions can grant a maximum of 1 skill at a time.
4. You can create potions with epic quality and skills corresponding to classes at level 20. You can create potions with epic quality and skills corresponding to classes at level 20. Your potions can give you a maximum of 3 skills at once (not a single potion, but you can possess up to 3 effects of those potions)
5. You can create potions with class skills of level 50, and your potions can give you up to 5 effects. You can create a potion that grants a legendary skill for 1 minute. Crafting such a potion is very expensive, and drinking more than one guarantees fatal poisoning.
Ritual - Complete a difficult task, defeat a strong enemy, or spend a long time in the company of a strong person while convincing everyone that you have a different class than you actually do.

#### Transformation Potions

3. You can create potions that allow you to transform into another sentient (playable) race of similar size. For this duration, you gain modifiers to the race's attributes, all default special abilities, and one special attribute. The potion lasts for up to one hour. After this time, or by drinking the antidote, you revert to your original form.
4. You can create potions that transform you into another creature that is not 1 level higher or lower than your original form, or 2 levels lower, but while in this form you have a debuff of -3 to Strength and Dexterity. The duration of potions for creatures of the same size changes for a day, for creatures of 1 size different for up to an hour, and for creatures of 2 sizes different, the duration is 6 turns/minutes.
5. The duration of your potions for playable races is unlimited; potions for creatures up to 2 different sizes have a duration of up to 1 day. Debuffs related to creature size are removed. You can create potions corresponding to giant creatures, but the duration of such a potion is limited to 3 minutes.
Ritual - Thoroughly investigate a powerful magical creature and obtain materials from that creature for your potion

## Whisper of the fallen
### description
1. You receive warnings/advice from dead souls. These take the form of incomprehensible whispers, sudden premonitions, temperature changes, goosebumps, etc. It operates passively.
2. Whispers become more understandable, you get more accurate information, not just a hunch.

### skill can evolve into three branches

#### Dark Shadow

3. You can target an enemy and make a contested willpower test. If you win, their own shadow materializes and begins attacking their original. Each person can only be attacked by one shadow at a time, and it is significantly weaker than the original.
4. You summon stronger shadows and can use skills on 3 people at once.
5. You can use the skill to create an almost identical shadow on 1 enemy above level 50 or on a group of weaker enemies up to 15. You are immune to this skill.
RItual - Kill 100 people with their own shadows and then defeat your own shadow.

#### Voice of the Fallen

3. You can actively speak to and see undead. You can instantly sense their presence and type within a 10-meter radius.
4. The undead are friendly towards each other. Their detection range increases to 25 meters. You intuitively sense their emotions and motivations.
5. You can demand obedience from the undead. You can sense undead and all information related to them within a 200-meter range.
RItual - Defeat an undead creature much stronger than you.
#### Shadow Monarch

3. Once you defeat an enemy, you can extract their soul/shadow to fight for you. If they're willing, you can do so without any problem. If they're hostile, you must make an opposed willpower test. You can conceal them and move them within your shadow. Shadows are significantly weaker than the original and can reach a maximum level of 20. To assimilate, the opponent must be a lower level than you. You can have up to five shadows.
4. Shadows have a lower power loss and can be leveled up to level 30. You can have up to 25 shadows.
5. All summons are successful if they are 15 levels lower than you. Shadows are nearly identical to the original. You can have up to 100 regular shadows and up to 5 commanders with awareness of level 50.
RItual - Defeat a tough enemy or complete a difficult task using only shadows.

There are many oaths , that differ between their descriptions and what  that paladin can choose one from
The oaths are the following :
1. Oath of Ancients
	Paladins of this oath follow these principles 
	 - **_Kindle the Light._** Through your acts of mercy, kindness, and forgiveness, kindle the light of hope in the world, beating back despair.
	- **_Shelter the Light._** Where there is good, beauty, love, and laughter in the world, stand against the wickedness that would swallow it. Where life flourishes, stand against the forces that would render it barren.
	- **_Preserve Your Own Light._** Delight in song and laughter, in beauty and art. If you allow the light to die in your own heart, you can't preserve it in the world.
	- **_Be the Light._** Be a glorious beacon for all who live in despair. Let the light of your joy and courage shine forth in all your deeds.
	they gain bonuses:
	 - smites can cause entangle on enemies
	 - bonus +2 dice to survival tests

	- **_Balance over reason_** No action shall interfere with the balance of the world, act only when the balance is disrupted.

   Paladins of this oath follow these principles:
	- **_Douse the Flame of Hope._** It is not enough to merely defeat an enemy in battle. Your victory must be so overwhelming that your enemies’ will to fight is shattered forever. A blade can end a life. Fear can end an empire.
